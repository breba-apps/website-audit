## About the company

Miden catalyzes onchain finance by combining programmable privacy, quantum-resistance, and unrivaled scalability.

Through pioneering ZK architecture, Miden enables local and private transaction execution, creating unmatched network efficiency.

### Miden Guardian

- Miden requires a fundamentally different way to coordinate, recover, and enforce policy. That is what Miden Guardian, Miden's institutional backend, provides.
- When relevant, reflect Miden Guardian as part of the institutional story.
- If the site or source material refers to "Guardian", do not confuse it with Guardian the publisher. Miden Guardian has nothing to do with Guardian the publisher.

## Audiences

- **Primary — institutional finance decision-makers.** Compliance officers, heads of digital assets, treasury leads, and product managers at banks, asset managers, payment processors, and fintechs evaluating blockchain rails for real-world financial operations. They arrive skeptical and risk-averse. They need to believe (1) Miden is actually compliant — not "privacy-preserving" in a way that creates regulatory exposure — and (2) there is a credible team and credible investors behind it.
- **Secondary — ZK/blockchain developers.** Protocol engineers, DeFi builders, and application developers evaluating Miden's technical stack to build on.

## Business goals

- **Primary — institutional pipeline.** Generate inbound from banks, fintechs, and asset managers: partnership inquiries, pilots, BD conversations.
- **Secondary — developer adoption.** Get builders onto testnet, into the docs, and building on Miden.

## Proof points and credibility signals

- $25M seed round; investors include a16z crypto, 1kx, Hack VC, Finality Capital Partners, Symbolic Capital, P2 Ventures.
- Founded by Bobbin Threadbare, Azeem Khan, and Dominik Schmid. Spun out of Polygon.
- Ecosystem and partner work: Circle/USDCx, Sempo, Qash, OpenZeppelin, LambdaClass, Gateway, Ligero, Para.
